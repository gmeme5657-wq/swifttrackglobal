Company policies

Clear service standards for a trusted shipping experience

Shipping Policy

Shipping & delivery standards

All shipments are processed on a first-in, first-out basis with routing based on destination, service level, and customs requirements. Transit timelines are estimated and may vary with weather, border processing, and carrier conditions.

Privacy Policy

Data protection & confidentiality

We collect only the information needed to process freight, provide tracking updates, and support communication. Customer data is protected, never sold, and used strictly for fulfillment, support, and operational improvement.

Terms of Service

Operational commitments

By using Swift Cargo Solutions, clients agree to provide accurate shipment details, pay all charges on time, and ensure goods are properly packaged and documented for transit. We reserve the right to refuse hazardous or prohibited items.

Refund Policy

Returns, claims & refunds

Refunds or adjustments are reviewed on a case-by-case basis for service failures, incorrect billing, or cancelled shipments within stated terms. Claims must be filed promptly with supporting documents and shipment details.
